Entry for make.fun's "The Word You'd Delete From the Dictionary" bounty ($2 in USDC, top 5 paid). The program writes one word to `/output/entry.txt`: **defenestration** — the act of throwing someone out of a window. Erase it and the language loses its only single word for the deed; the crime loses its name and has to be described in a whole sentence. A grand Latinate mouthful for one very specific act — fresh, instantly understood, and funny the moment the word is gone.

